# Positioning and comparison

This Actor is a lightweight, query-driven business discovery and extraction workflow. Apollo, ZoomInfo, and Lusha are established sales intelligence products with their own data sources, features, coverage, and terms. This repository does not provide a feature-by-feature or quality benchmark against them.

| Consideration | This Actor | Commercial sales intelligence platforms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery approach | Search-engine results for user-provided terms and locations | Platform-specific datasets and product capabilities; verify details with each vendor |
| Contact output | Publicly surfaced, email-like strings and links when present in the requested page | Product-specific contact data and verification features; check current vendor documentation |
| Operation | Run an Apify Actor; inspect/export dataset output | Use each platform's own interface, API, plan, and terms |
| Best fit | Flexible web research and custom automation workflows | Teams evaluating maintained prospecting databases and their bundled workflows |

Choose based on coverage, permitted use, data quality, freshness, integration requirements, and total cost. The Actor is an alternative workflow for business discovery, not a claim of equivalent coverage, contact verification, or deliverability.
